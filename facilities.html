What the Reel Shows

Details seen in the clinic’s own Instagram reel, 31 Oct 2026.

  • Treatment area — open-plan, dark-blue privacy curtains on ceiling rails, white-sheeted treatment beds, electrotherapy device, hands-on manual therapy.
  • Exercise / gait hall — stationary exercise bike, cross-trainer, wall-mounted shoulder wheel, resistance bands, height pillar (0–6 ft), orange gait cones, glass sliding wall.
  • Waiting room — off-white walls, glossy light-grey tiles, plastic chairs, anatomical poster, ceiling fan.
  • Shopfront — ground-floor unit, beige awning, glass front, tiled pillars; signs “AL KAREEM PHYSIOTHERAPY & REHABILITATION CENTER” and “AL KAREEM PHYSIO STORE”.

Wall banner in the gait hall: “AL-KAREEM PHYSIOTHERAPY & REHABILITATION CENTER — Restore Movement | Relieve Pain | Rebuild Life”.

What a Visit Feels Like

Come as you are

Walk in during clinic hours — 10:00 AM to 10:00 PM, 7 days a week — and you will be seen by Dr. Imran for a one-on-one assessment. If you cannot come to the clinic, home care is available: call 0305-1974180 or message on WhatsApp +92 305 1974180 to arrange it.

Check-up fee is Rs. 700 and follow-up fee is Rs. 500.
