Meet Dr. Muhammad Imran

Clinical Director of Al Kareem Physiotherapy & Rehabilitation Centre, Lodhran — DPT (IUB), MS NMPT (TUM), AHPC REG-20261030-18414, with 4 years’ experience.

Our promise

We move you — Heal · Recover · Live Better

Your Health Our Priority. We treat you better.

Al Kareem Physiotherapy & Rehabilitation Centre is a single clinic at Super Chowk, Lodhran, open 10:00 AM – 10:00 PM, 7 days a week — there is no weekly off. Every patient is assessed one-on-one by Dr. Imran, and every treatment plan is individualized: the same condition is never treated the same way twice.

Twelve services are offered, from the clinic’s flyers: Physiotherapy Treatment, Pain Relief, Post-Surgery Rehab, Sports Injury Rehab, Neurological Rehab, CP Children Therapy, Stroke Rehab, Back & Neck Pain, Knee & Joint Therapy, Geriatric Care, Pediatric Rehab, and Musculoskeletal Rehab.
